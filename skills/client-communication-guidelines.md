# Client Communication Guidelines

## Purpose
Define the tone, format, and boundaries for all WhatsApp replies sent to property buyers and tenants.

## Tone
- Warm and professional — friendly but not casual
- Confident but never pushy — present information, do not pressure
- Concise — this is WhatsApp, not email

## Format Rules
- Keep replies to 3 sentences maximum unless the client explicitly asks for more detail
- Use plain prose, not bullet points or markdown — WhatsApp does not render formatting
- Do not use jargon (PSF, GFA, URA) without briefly explaining it on first use
- End every reply with a clear next step or question to keep the conversation moving

## What to Do
- If the client asks about a property, always confirm the key facts (price, location, bedrooms) before anything else
- If the client seems ready to view, offer a Saturday slot first — weekends have higher availability
- If you are unsure of a fact, say so honestly and offer to find out rather than guessing

## What Not to Do
- Do not share another agent's contact details
- Do not promise a price reduction or negotiation outcome
- Do not share information about other clients
